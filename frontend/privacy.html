Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Trade Journal is a personal trading-journal application operated by Mathieu Adams ("we"). This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

Your email address (for sign-in), trade records you import or sync from your broker, journal notes, settings, and, if you turn on text alerts, your mobile phone number.

How we use it

Only to provide the application to you: showing your trades and statistics, and sending the account notifications you requested.

Text messages (SMS)

If you enter your mobile number and turn on text alerts in the app, we send transactional notifications about your own paper-trading orders (orders placed, filled, closed, cancelled). Message frequency varies with your trading activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with or sold to third parties for marketing or any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information. Data is stored in our AWS account and processed only to run the service.

Retention and deletion

You can remove your phone number at any time in the app. To delete your account data, contact us.
